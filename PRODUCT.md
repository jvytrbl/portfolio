# Product

register: brand

## Purpose

Personal portfolio and engineering showcase for Muhammad Hakimi, a junior software engineer in
Seremban, Malaysia. It exists to win interviews for full-stack and integration roles in KL and
Selangor by proving real production impact, not by listing skills.

## Users

| Persona                          | What they need in the first 30 seconds                                              |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Technical recruiter / HR         | Role, location, availability, and one undeniable piece of proof.                    |
| Hiring manager / senior engineer | Evidence of architectural thinking: case studies with problem, design, and outcome. |
| Potential client / collaborator  | What he builds and how to reach him.                                                |

## Positioning

"I turn messy business workflows into production systems." The flagship proof is the Jotex
defect-ticketing system: WhatsApp groups and manual logs replaced by one system used daily by four
departments, with two production apps and a 90,000+ record data browser.

## Brand and tone

- Concept: **an engineering journal**. Premium, academic, editorial; "university press", not
  "developer template". Case studies read like feature articles.
- Plain, confident, specific. Numbers over adjectives. No hype.
- Ink edition (#070429 indigo, ivory type, crimson details) is the default; a warm "paper"
  edition is the light theme.
- One terminal moment only: the `impact.log` artifact inside the Jotex case study.

## Structure

A real multi-page site: Home (cinematic cover and scroll story) → Work (archive + case
studies) → About (profile, career, credentials, toolkit) → Contact.

## Anti-references

- Generic "terminal portfolio" templates: ~/paths on every heading, `cd ~` jokes, blinking cursors
  everywhere, typewriters that hide the content.
- One long landing page where every section competes; same-template sections repeated.
- SaaS hero-metric templates, gradient text, glass cards, identical card grids.

## Principles

1. Lead with proof from real work.
2. Every number must come from the résumé or case studies; never invent metrics.
3. Accessible by default: WCAG 2.1 AA, 44px targets, no infinite motion.
4. Free-tier infrastructure only.
