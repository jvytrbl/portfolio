## Problem

Defect reports at a fabric manufacturer lived in WhatsApp groups and manual logs. Nothing was
traceable end to end, and every department kept its own partial picture.

## Solution

As the sole engineer I built one ticketing system now used daily by **sales, warehouse, accounts,
and purchasing**: a mobile-first PWA for field sales and a desktop dashboard for operations, on a
shared real-time Supabase backend with Azure AD single sign-on and role-based access across
business units.

```txt
Field sales PWA ─┐
                 ├──► Supabase (Postgres + real-time sync)
Ops dashboard ───┘            │
                              ▼
         ERP pipeline ──► Microsoft Dynamics Business Central
                          (service orders · credit notes)
```

An ERP pipeline creates service orders and credit notes in Business Central automatically. When a
90,000+ record data browser began crashing browsers, I moved it to server-side pagination.
