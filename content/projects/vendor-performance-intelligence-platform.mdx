## Problem

Vendor reports had to be trustworthy enough to act on. Two risks stood out: incomplete records
being read as poor performance, and AI summaries inventing or miscalculating figures.

## Solution

Starting from a formal requirements document signed off by a senior engineer, I modelled three
benchmarks (delivery time, pricing, and order accuracy) with deliberate handling of _missing_
versus _zero_ data. Every number is calculated by the application; the Gemini API only writes the
narrative, and a validation step checks its output against the source data before anything is
finalised. Retries with backoff, a circuit breaker, schema-constrained output, and full
interaction logging keep it reliable when the AI provider isn't.

## Outcome

A tested, secured system I provisioned alone on Azure (AD, SQL Database, Key Vault, least-privilege
RBAC), verified by unit and integration tests in Vitest, end-to-end tests in Playwright, and
Stryker mutation testing to prove the tests catch real bugs.
